Common Questions

Find quick answers to the most frequently asked questions about using SellSync.

How does sales and inventory sync work?

+

Sales and inventory update instantly with every transaction, so you always have accurate, real-time data—no manual updates needed.

Can I oversee multiple locations together?

+

Yes, manage all stores, warehouses, and staff from one dashboard with customizable access for your team.

What analytics insights are available?

+

Get sales trends, inventory forecasts, and practical recommendations to help you optimize your business operations.

Is this platform right for small businesses?

+

Yes. The system is flexible for single stores and scalable for larger businesses, with tools for every size.

How secure is SellSync?

+

SellSync uses enterprise-level security protocols, including encryption, secure login, and regular backups, to keep your business data safe.

Can I give my team restricted access?

+

Yes. Role-based permissions allow you to control who can view, edit, or manage sales, inventory, and analytics data.
