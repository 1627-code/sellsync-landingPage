Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create my first campaign?

To create your first campaign, log in to your SellSyn account, navigate to the Campaigns tab, and click "Create New Campaign". Follow the step-by-step wizard to set up your campaign name, target audience, email content, and schedule.

Why are my emails not sending?

There could be several reasons why your emails aren't sending: your email account might not be properly connected, you might have reached your daily sending limit, or there could be an issue with your email content. Check your email integration settings and review any error messages in the campaign dashboard.

How do I connect my email account?

To connect your email account, go to Settings > Email Integrations and select your email provider. Follow the authentication steps to grant SellSyn access to send emails on your behalf. We support Gmail, Outlook, and most other major email providers.

How do I import leads?

You can import leads by going to the Leads tab and clicking "Import Leads". You can upload a CSV file or connect directly to your CRM. Make sure your CSV file includes columns for email addresses and any other relevant contact information.

How do I upgrade my plan?

To upgrade your plan, go to Settings > Billing and select the plan that best fits your needs. Follow the prompts to complete the upgrade process. Your new plan will take effect immediately, and you'll be billed prorated for the current billing period.
